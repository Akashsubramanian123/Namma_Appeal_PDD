Date of Hearing: 18 August 2026

Appellant: Akash S.
Respondent: CPIO, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways

ORDER
1. The appellant filed a Second Appeal under Section 19(3) of the RTI Act, 2005, aggrieved by the FAA's order upholding the CPIO’s decision to deny information regarding the structural audit report of the civic infrastructure project.
2. The CPIO had denied the information claiming exemption under Section 8(1)(d) [Commercial Confidence]. 
3. The Commission observes that the structural integrity of public roads directly affects the safety of citizens. The disclosure of this audit report is overwhelmingly in the larger public interest, which outweighs the protection of any commercial confidence of the private contractor involved.
4. DECISION: The appeal is ALLOWED. The CPIO's reliance on Section 8(1)(d) is legally untenable. The CPIO is directed to furnish a certified copy of the complete structural audit report to the appellant within 15 days. Failure to comply will attract penalty proceedings under Section 20(1) of the RTI Act.
