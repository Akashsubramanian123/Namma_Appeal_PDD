Subject: Rejection of RTI Application.

1. Reference is invited to your RTI application dated 20 July 2026.
2. In your application, you have stated: "Please investigate the unauthorized parking of vehicles on my street and inform me what strict disciplinary action has been taken against the local traffic inspector for negligence."
3. Under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, 2005, the PIO is only mandated to provide existing records. The RTI Act is not a forum for grievance redressal, demanding investigations, or compelling the public authority to take punitive action.
4. Hence, your application is rejected.
