Subject: Disposal of First Appeal under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005.

1. The appellant filed a First Appeal against the CPIO’s reply dated 5th August 2026. 
2. In the original RTI application, the appellant sought the annual performance appraisal reports (APAR), medical leave records, and internal disciplinary memos of a third-party government employee.
3. The CPIO denied the information citing Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, 2005.
4. Having examined the appeal, the FAA concurs with the CPIO. The information sought qualifies as personal information of a third party, the disclosure of which has no relationship to any public activity or interest, and would cause an unwarranted invasion of the privacy of the individual.
5. DECISION: The appeal is DISMISSED. The CPIO's decision to deny the information under Section 8(1)(j) is upheld.
