Subject: Order in First Appeal under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005.

1. This order disposes of the First Appeal dated 15th July 2026 filed by the appellant, Akash S., against the deemed refusal of his RTI application dated 10th June 2026 by the Public Information Officer (PIO).
2. The appellant had sought information regarding the timeline and allocated budget for the repair of the main access road connecting the university campus to the state highway.
3. Upon reviewing the records, it is observed that the PIO failed to furnish the requested information within the mandatory 30-day period stipulated under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act, 2005. No justifiable reason for this delay was provided on record.
4. DECISION: The appeal is ALLOWED. The PIO is hereby directed to provide the complete, point-wise information to the appellant, free of charge as per Section 7(6) of the Act, within 7 working days from the receipt of this order.
