Subject: Information sought under RTI Act, 2005.

1. We have received your RTI application seeking the detailed audit reports and internal communications between the RBI and a private commercial bank regarding their specific client loan defaults.
2. The information sought is held by the Reserve Bank of India in a fiduciary capacity. Disclosure of such information would harm the competitive position of the third party and breach trust.
3. Therefore, the information is strictly exempt from disclosure under Section 8(1)(e) and Section 8(1)(d) of the RTI Act, 2005.
4. The application is disposed of accordingly.
