Date of Decision: 22 August 2026

Appellant: Akash S.
Respondent: CPIO, Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

ORDER
1. The appellant filed an RTI application seeking answers to hypothetical scenarios regarding the UPSC Civil Services Examination syllabus, specifically asking the CPIO to "clarify why certain Ancient Indian History topics are prioritized" and to "provide a subjective analysis of the expected question patterns for the upcoming year."
2. The CPIO responded stating that the queries do not fall under the definition of "information" as per Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, 2005.
3. The Commission notes that under the RTI Act, a public authority is only required to provide information that already exists and is held in material form. The CPIO is not required to answer interrogatory questions, deduce conclusions, provide subjective clarifications, or create new information.
4. DECISION: The appeal is DISMISSED. The Commission finds no infirmity in the reply provided by the CPIO. No further intervention of the Commission is required in this matter.
